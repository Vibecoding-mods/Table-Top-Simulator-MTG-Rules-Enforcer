# Wire Forge's hidden exile links into TTS

Commander's odd zone cards all fall into a few families, and one question sorts them. **What keeps the exiled card tied to something, and who may act on it?** Valki, Hostage Taker and Banishing Light use one-shot "until" durations (CR 610.3). Their cards come back instantly, to the zone they left, and are never exiled at all if the exiler is gone first. O-Ring, Fiend Hunter and Detention Sphere use a two-trigger pattern instead, and the same removal exiles the target **forever**. Arcane Bombardment, Tibalt, Ashiok, Bomat Courier and imprint cards hold "exiled with" links (CR 607) that belong to one object instance and are cut when it changes zones (CR 400.7). Gonti, Thief of Sanity, impulse draw, adventure, foretell, plot, suspend, warp and airbend use **permissions** that belong to a player and expire on a clock, not to a permanent. Dauthi Voidwalker, Karn and Grolnok track state with **counters** on the exiled card. Similar traps sit in bounce, flicker, copy, sacrifice, mill, phasing and attach. The engine side is in better shape than the table side. Forge already models every link (`ExiledWith`, `UntilLeavesBattlefield`, `ExiledCards`, `ImprintedCards`, `PlayerMayLook`, `MayPlay`, `Foretold`, prepared spells). The Manabrew snapshot sends **none of the link or permission data**, only status flags, counters and correctly redacted face-down stubs. The most valuable work is therefore a small set of new `CardDto` fields in the Java harness, plus three always-visible cues on the TTS table:
- a tether or pile at the exiler;
- a "playable" row beside the hand;
- a per-player visibility marker on face-down exile.

## Two return mechanisms decide whether exiled cards ever come back

The plain case is one-way exile. The first family adds a return, and the exact wording decides how it works. Single-ability "until" cards (Banishing Light, Banisher Priest, Hostage Taker, Grasp of Fate, Brutal Cathar, Valki, Kitesail Freebooter, Brain Maggot, Intellect Devourer) create **two one-shot effects**. The second returns the object "to its previous zone" immediately after the specified event, and **nothing happens in between, not even state-based actions** ([CR](https://media.wizards.com/2026/downloads/MagicCompRules%2020260925.txt); [Scryfall: Hostage Taker](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/231/hostage-taker)). If the exiler has already left when the exile would happen, "the object doesn't move" (CR 610.3a/b). Two-trigger cards (Oblivion Ring, Fiend Hunter, Journey to Nowhere, Detention Sphere, and the champion keyword) put an ordinary leaves-the-battlefield trigger on the stack. Kill the exiler in response to its ETB and the return trigger resolves first and does nothing, then the ETB resolves "and exile[s] the targeted nonland permanent forever" ([Scryfall: Oblivion Ring](https://scryfall.com/card/mm2/29/oblivion-ring); [Scryfall: Fiend Hunter](https://scryfall.com/card/hoc/167/fiend-hunter)). The difference also shows in multiplayer. If a Hostage Taker's controller leaves the game, the card returns, because the return "isn't an ability that goes on the stack." If a Fiend Hunter's controller loses, the creature "remains exiled indefinitely" ([Scryfall: Hostage Taker](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/231/hostage-taker); [Scryfall: Fiend Hunter](https://scryfall.com/card/hoc/167/fiend-hunter)). Skyclave Apparition looks like a member of this family but is not. Its exile is permanent, and its leave trigger only gives the owner an X/X Illusion ([Scryfall: Skyclave Apparition](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/173/skyclave-apparition)).

Valki, God of Lies shows how a familiar keyword hides an unfamiliar destination. Valki exiles creature cards from opponents' **hands** "until Valki leaves the battlefield," so CR 610.3 sends them back to their owners' **hands**, not the battlefield. The same applies to Freebooter, Brain Maggot, Deep-Cavern Bat and Intellect Devourer ([Scryfall: Valki](https://scryfall.com/card/khm/114/valki-god-of-lies-tibalt-cosmic-impostor); [CR](https://media.wizards.com/2026/downloads/MagicCompRules%2020260925.txt)). Valki's {X} copy ability chooses the card on resolution and does nothing if no exiled card has mana value X. Once Valki becomes a copy he loses that activated ability, and any object copying Valki becomes whatever Valki is copying ([Scryfall: Valki](https://scryfall.com/card/khm/114/valki-god-of-lies-tibalt-cosmic-impostor)). Becoming a copy is not a zone change (CR 707.4), so Valki's "until" duration should keep running while he copies something. That follows from CR 400.7 and 610.3, but no Valki-specific ruling confirms it. Transforming is not leaving either: Brutal Cathar's exiled card stays exiled while Cathar is Moonrage Brute ([Scryfall: Brutal Cathar](https://scryfall.com/card/mid/7/brutal-cathar-moonrage-brute)). Phasing is not leaving: a phased-out Banishing Light does **not** release its card ([Scryfall: Teferi's Protection](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/32/teferis-protection)).

Returned objects are new objects under their **owner's** control (CR 610.3c, 400.7). Counters cease to exist (CR 122.2). Auras go to the graveyard and Equipment stays behind unattached. An exiled token never comes back. A returning Aura with no legal object "remains in exile for the rest of the game" ([Scryfall: Banisher Priest](https://scryfall.com/card/moc/173/banisher-priest)). Detention Sphere also exiles untargeted same-name permanents, lands included ([Scryfall: Detention Sphere](https://scryfall.com/card/rtr/155/detention-sphere)). If a linked exile trigger is copied with Strionic Resonator, "the exiled card" means every card exiled, so both come back (CR 607.3; [Scryfall: Strionic Resonator](https://scryfall.com/card/moc/384/strionic-resonator)). The table link must therefore be one-to-many.

Commanders add one more step. Under current CR 903.9a, a commander put into exile may go to the command zone as a **one-time state-based action**. The owner chooses one of two outcomes. The commander goes home and Banishing Light "loses track of it," or it stays in exile and returns later. "No longer can players get the best of both worlds" ([CR](https://media.wizards.com/2026/downloads/MagicCompRules%2020260925.txt); [Cranial Insertion](https://www.cranial-insertion.com/articles/3314)). A 2019 judge tip claiming Banishing Light tracks a commander into the command zone describes the pre-2020 rule and is obsolete ([Magic Judges](https://blogs.magicjudges.org/rulestips/?p=6656)).

## "Exiled with" links die with the object; permissions outlive it

CR 607 links abilities on one object. "The exiled card" and "cards exiled with [this]" refer only to cards exiled by that same object's linking ability. **Imprint is now an ability word with no rules meaning**; the link comes from 607 alone ([CR](https://media.wizards.com/2026/downloads/MagicCompRules%2020260925.txt)). The link is lost on any zone change. When Arcane Bombardment leaves, "the exiled cards remain in exile." A returned or second Bombardment won't copy them ([Scryfall: Arcane Bombardment](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/154/arcane-bombardment)). A new Ashiok, Nightmare Weaver "is a new object" and can't use the old one's cards ([Scryfall: Ashiok](https://scryfall.com/card/ths/188/ashiok-nightmare-weaver)). Each Bomat Courier "has its own cache," and if it leaves before you use it the cache stays face down forever ([Scryfall: Bomat Courier](https://scryfall.com/card/kld/199/bomat-courier)). Some imprint cards have their own quirks. Chrome Mox makes no mana with nothing imprinted or a colorless card imprinted, and never makes {C}. Isochron Scepter can still copy if the Scepter leaves while its activation is on the stack, but not if the imprinted card leaves exile. Mimic Vat sends previously imprinted cards to the graveyard when it imprints a new one, and its token copies whatever is exiled **at resolution** ([Scryfall: Chrome Mox](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/240/chrome-mox); [Scryfall: Isochron Scepter](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/264/isochron-scepter); [Scryfall: Mimic Vat](https://scryfall.com/card/ncc/372/mimic-vat)). Hideaway (CR 702.75) exiles face down. Any player who has controlled the hideaway permanent since then may look at the card, so the set of players allowed to look only grows ([Scryfall: Mosswort Bridge](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/379/mosswort-bridge)).

Tibalt, Cosmic Impostor is the exception that proves the rule. His emblem is a separate object linked to him under CR 607.1d. It works for cards exiled by "that specific Tibalt … even after that Tibalt leaves the battlefield," and a new Tibalt brings a new emblem with its own pile ([Scryfall: Valki // Tibalt](https://scryfall.com/card/khm/114/valki-god-of-lies-tibalt-cosmic-impostor)). Gonti, Lord of Luxury and Thief of Sanity carry the same idea further. Their "for as long as it remains exiled" permission belongs to the **player**. It survives the creature dying, it does not pass to someone who steals the creature, and if the player leaves the game the card stays face down forever and "No player may look at it" ([Scryfall: Gonti](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/135/gonti-lord-of-luxury); [Scryfall: Thief of Sanity](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/243/thief-of-sanity)). Forge models it this way: Gonti's permission is a separate permanent Effect with `MayPlay$ True` and `ForgetOnMoved$ Exile`, not a static on Gonti ([Forge gonti_lord_of_luxury.txt](https://github.com/Card-Forge/forge/blob/master/forge-gui/res/cardsfolder/g/gonti_lord_of_luxury.txt)). The practical rule follows. Permissions worded "for as long as it remains exiled" (Gonti, Hostage Taker, Thief, Praetor's Grasp, Opposition Agent) are player-owned and last until the card leaves exile. Permissions worded as an ability of the permanent (Urianger, Lobelia, Ashiok, Valgavoth, Bombardment, hideaway lands) die with that object. The table must show "linked to" and "playable by" as **two independent indicators**.

Counter-marked exile is not linked at all. The counter is the only record. Dauthi Voidwalker's sacrifice ability picks any opponent-owned card with a void counter. Karn, Scion of Urza's −1 takes any card you own with a silver counter, and Karn is the only Commander-legal card that mentions silver counters. Grolnok explicitly works with croak-countered cards from "an earlier incarnation" ([Scryfall: Dauthi Voidwalker](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/176/dauthi-voidwalker); [Scryfall: Karn](https://scryfall.com/card/dom/1/karn-scion-of-urza); [Scryfall: Grolnok](https://scryfall.com/card/vow/238/grolnok-the-omnivore)). Counters vanish on any zone change, including being exiled again from exile (CR 122.2, 406.7). That means the TTS exile area must **render counters on exiled cards**.

## Permission-based exile runs on clocks, wording and timing

Impulse draw gives the controller of the effect a timed right to **play** or only **cast** specific exiled cards. Normal timing and land-drop rules still apply, and unplayed cards stay exiled forever. Light Up the Stage lasts until the end of your next turn. Jeska's Will and Laelia last this turn. Ragavan's card can only be **cast**, so "If you exile a land card, you can't play that card" ([Scryfall: Light Up the Stage](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/166/light-up-the-stage); [Scryfall: Jeska's Will](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/156/jeskas-will); [Scryfall: Ragavan](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/138/ragavan-nimble-pilferer)). A card played this way becomes a new object and can't be played again from that permission even if it returns to exile ([Scryfall: Laelia](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/246/laelia-the-blade-reforged)). Mana flexibility depends on wording. "Mana of any **type**" (Gonti, Hostage Taker) lets colorless mana pay colored symbols, because colorless is one of the six types. "Any **color**" (Tibalt, Opposition Agent) only swaps colors among themselves. CR 609.4b says this changes only how the cost is paid ([CR](https://media.wizards.com/2026/downloads/MagicCompRules%2020260925.txt); [Scryfall: Gonti](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/135/gonti-lord-of-luxury)). No ruling states the Sol-Ring-can't-pay-{R} case for Tibalt explicitly; it follows from the definitions. Cast-during-resolution effects ignore type-based timing and end when the ability finishes resolving. These include Etali, Hellcarver Demon, Bombardment's copies, Ashiok, Nightmare Muse's −7, cascade, discover and madness. Etali adds limits: no alternative costs, X = 0, and "Any cards not cast … remain in exile" ([Scryfall: Etali](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/137/etali-primal-conqueror-etali-primal-sickness); [Scryfall: Hellcarver Demon](https://scryfall.com/card/roe/113/hellcarver-demon)). Ashiok's −7 can take **any** face-up opponent-owned exiled card, "not just those exiled by Ashiok." That can pull cards out of an opponent's Banishing Light or Dauthi pile, so selection must not be limited to the effect's own pile ([Scryfall: Ashiok, Nightmare Muse](https://scryfall.com/card/thb/208/ashiok-nightmare-muse)).

Some exile permissions go to the **owner**, not the exiler. Elite Spellbinder (+{2}), Soul Partition (+{2} only for opponents), plot (CR 702.170d), warp (CR 702.185a) and airbend (CR 701.65a, cast for {2}) all let the card's owner play it later ([Scryfall: Elite Spellbinder](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/17/elite-spellbinder); [CR](https://media.wizards.com/2026/downloads/MagicCompRules%2020260925.txt)). Casting an opponent's card makes you controller but never owner. The card later goes to the owner's graveyard or hand (CR 400.3), and is exiled if the caster leaves (CR 800.4a). A stolen commander keeps its designation (CR 903.3). Cast from exile it pays no tax, because tax counts only command-zone casts (CR 903.8). It still deals commander damage, which can kill its own owner (CR 903.10a). These Commander conclusions follow from the CR. No card-specific ruling confirms them.

The keyword exile states differ in a few columns. The table below condenses CR 715, 702.143, 702.170, 702.62, 702.88, 702.35, 702.85, 701.57, 702.75, 702.167, 702.185, 701.65, 722 and 702.192 with the cited rulings ([CR](https://media.wizards.com/2026/downloads/MagicCompRules%2020260925.txt); [Scryfall: Bonecrusher Giant](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/781/bonecrusher-giant-stomp); [Scryfall: Saw It Coming](https://scryfall.com/card/khm/76/saw-it-coming); [Scryfall: Aven Interrupter](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/4/aven-interrupter); [Scryfall: Ancestral Vision](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/144/ancestral-vision); [Scryfall: Ephemerate](https://scryfall.com/card/mh1/7/ephemerate); [Wizards: EOE release notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/edge-of-eternities-release-notes); [Wizards: SOS release notes](https://media.wizards.com/2026/downloads/SOS_Release_Notes_FwhcBWdFIE/EN_MTGSOS_ReleaseNotes_20260410.pdf)).

Face-down exile is its own visibility problem. Exile is public by default. A face-down exiled card has no characteristics. Only permitted players may look, and once permitted they keep looking until it leaves exile. Separate face-down piles "based on when they were exiled and how" are required (CR 406.3, 406.4). Praetor's Grasp spells out that the card's **owner** may not look ([Scryfall: Praetor's Grasp](https://scryfall.com/card/nph/71/praetors-grasp)). Foretold cards must be revealed when their owner leaves and at game end (CR 702.143f). The notes found no general rule that reveals other face-down exiled cards when they leave exile, except by being cast face up (CR 406.3a). Face-down exile is also unrelated to face-down permanents (CR 110.5d).

## Bounce, flicker, copy, sacrifice, mill, phasing and attach each hide one owner-or-object trap

The same two ideas, **owner versus controller** and **new object versus same object**, explain most mistakes outside exile.

Bounce and tuck always go to the owner's zone (CR 400.3). Tokens reach the zone and then cease to exist. A commander headed to hand or library goes to the command zone through a **replacement** (CR 903.9b), so it never arrives there. Graveyard and exile instead use the after-the-fact state-based action (CR 903.9a) ([CR](https://media.wizards.com/2026/downloads/MagicCompRules%2020260925.txt)). Hallowed Burial lets each player order their own cards secretly, regardless of who controlled them ([Scryfall: Hallowed Burial](https://scryfall.com/card/cn2/91/hallowed-burial)).

Flicker always makes a new object: counters vanish, Auras go to the graveyard, Equipment falls off, and the object leaves combat and loses its old targets. What varies is the timing and the controller. Ephemerate, Eldrazi Displacer and Brago return the card immediately under its **owner's** control. Restoration Angel, Cloudshift and Conjurer's Closet return it under **your** control, so a Threatened creature stays yours: "it won't return to its previous controller." Flickerwisp returns at the next end step even if Flickerwisp is gone ([Scryfall: Restoration Angel](https://scryfall.com/card/inr/38/restoration-angel); [Scryfall: Flickerwisp](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/11/flickerwisp); [Scryfall: Cloudshift](https://scryfall.com/card/tle/152/cloudshift)).

Copies of spells are **not cast** (CR 707.10), so storm and "whenever you cast" triggers ignore them. A copy of a permanent spell becomes a token. A copy of a commander is not a commander: "If Sakashima is not your commander, it remains not your commander even if it's copying your commander" ([Scryfall: Sakashima](https://scryfall.com/card/cmr/89/sakashima-of-a-thousand-faces)). "Becomes a copy" effects (Valki, Mirage Mirror) cause no ETB and no LTB (CR 707.4).

"Dies" covers destroy, sacrifice, state-based actions and the legend rule. Indestructible and regeneration stop only destroy (CR 700.4, 701.8b, 701.21a). A commander really dies before it moves to the command zone, so Blood Artist triggers. An AI summary claiming otherwise reflected pre-2020 rules ([Commander Legends Release Notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/feature/commander-legends-release-notes-2020-11-06)).

Mill is CR **701.17** in this edition, not the old 701.13. Only the word "mill" counts: "If a player is instructed to put a card into their graveyard without using the word 'mill,' Bruvac's ability doesn't apply" ([Scryfall: Bruvac](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/35/bruvac-the-grandiloquent)). Surveil is not mill, and mill is not discard.

Phasing is not a zone change. Tokens survive, counters and attachments stay, and no ETB or LTB triggers fire. Oubliette is an "until leaves" phasing, and its creature phases back in tapped ([CR](https://media.wizards.com/2026/downloads/MagicCompRules%2020260925.txt); [Scryfall: Oubliette](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/100/oubliette)).

When the host leaves:
- an Aura goes to the graveyard;
- Equipment stays unattached;
- a bestowed Aura becomes a creature (CR 702.103f);
- a reconfigure Equipment becomes a creature again.

Stealing the host never steals the attachment (CR 303.4e, 301.5d; [Scryfall: Control Magic](https://scryfall.com/card/cma/34/control-magic)).

Transformed DFCs return front face up and keep the front face's mana value. Transforming keeps the same object, so commander damage keeps accumulating (CR 712.8e, 712.14, 712.18).

The errors players make most often, each sourced above:

| Edge case | What actually happens |
|---|---|
| Exiler removed in response to its ETB | O-Ring/Fiend Hunter/Journey/D-Sphere: target exiled **forever**. Banishing Light/Priest/Hostage Taker/Valki: **nothing** exiled |
| Timing of an "until" return | Immediate, no SBA gap, can't be responded to; two-trigger returns use the stack and can be Stifled |
| Valki / Freebooter / Brain Maggot return | To the owner's **hand** |
| Tokens, counters, Auras under linked exile | Tokens gone; counters gone; Auras to graveyard; Equipment stays |
| Exiler flickered or recast | New object, empty pile; old cards orphaned (except Tibalt emblem, Gonti/Thief permissions, counter-marked cards) |
| Ragavan/Hostage Taker/Gonti (Lord of Luxury)/Thief lands | "Cast" can't play lands; "play" (Gonti, Canny Acquisitor; Tibalt; impulse) can, on your turn with a land drop |
| Any type vs any color | Colorless pays colored only under "any type" |
| Exiled or bounced commander | Exile/graveyard: one SBA choice; hand/library: replacement. Banishing Light can't have it both ways |
| Stolen commander cast from exile | No tax; still commander damage |
| Restoration Angel on a Threatened creature | You keep it; Ephemerate gives it back |
| Phased-out Banishing Light | Exiled card stays exiled |
| Countered flashback/jump-start spell | Exile, not graveyard |

## Forge knows every link; the relay strips them before TTS sees them

Forge stores the exiled-with link on both sides. The exiled card holds `exiledWith`, `exiledBy` and `exiledSA`. The host holds `exiledCards`, `untilLeavesBattlefield`, `imprintedCards` and `encodedCards` ([Forge Card.java](https://github.com/Card-Forge/forge/blob/master/forge-game/src/main/java/forge/game/card/Card.java); [SpellAbilityEffect.java](https://github.com/Card-Forge/forge/blob/master/forge-game/src/main/java/forge/game/ability/SpellAbilityEffect.java)). The network `CardView` already publishes `ExiledWith`, `ExiledCards`, `UntilLeavesBattlefield`, `ImprintedCards`, `EncodedCards`, `PlayerMayLook`, `Facedown`, `Foretold`, `Counters`, `HiddenId` and `PreparedSpell` ([TrackableProperty.java](https://github.com/Card-Forge/forge/blob/master/forge-game/src/main/java/forge/trackable/TrackableProperty.java)). Play permission is the exception. `MayPlay` is an engine-only `Map<StaticAbility, CardPlayOption>` carrying the players, free-cast, any-type and any-color flags, usually granted by a command-zone Effect card. It reaches a UI only as the computed "Playable Zone Cards" list (`Player.getCardsIn(ZoneType.Flashback)`) ([CardPlayOption.java](https://github.com/Card-Forge/forge/blob/master/forge-game/src/main/java/forge/game/card/CardPlayOption.java); [Player.java](https://github.com/Card-Forge/forge/blob/master/forge-game/src/main/java/forge/game/player/Player.java)). `isPlotted`, `isOnAdventure`, `hasSuspend` and `isWarped` are engine methods with no `CardView` property. Adventure is modelled as "exiled with itself."

Forge's own maintainers added faded "ghost" copies of held cards to the permanent in June 2026 because linked exile was "a recurring source of confusion" ([Forge PR #10873](https://github.com/Card-Forge/forge/pull/10873)). XMage auto-opens one window per source-keyed exile zone ([XMage GamePanel.java](https://github.com/magefree/mage/blob/master/Mage.Client/src/main/java/mage/client/game/GamePanel.java)). Arena pulls playable exiled cards into a row to the right of the hand ([MTGA Assistant](https://mtgaassistant.net/Article/MTG-Arena-Match-Playing-Peculiarities-Guide)). All three engine-backed clients therefore converge on two cues: **a link at the exiler and a playable row near the hand**. Arena's 2024–2026 patch notes still keep fixing face-down visibility and castability bugs ([MTGAZone](https://mtgazone.com/2023-36-0-patch-notes/); [Arena 2026.55.0](https://mtgarena-support.wizards.com/hc/en-us/articles/45254706663956-Patch-Notes-2026-55-0)). That argues for taking both facts from the engine on every update and never computing them in Lua.

Manabrew's harness serializes exile with face-down entries the viewer can't see reduced to `{visibility:"hidden", id}`. It uses Forge's own `canBeShownTo`, so **redaction is already correct** ([manabrew InteractiveSnapshotExtractor.java](https://github.com/witchesofthehill/manabrew/blob/main/forge-harness/src/main/java/forge/harness/host/InteractiveSnapshotExtractor.java)). Upstream `CardDto` adds `isFaceDown`, `isPlotted`, `isWarpExiled`, `isMadnessExiled`, `isSuspended`, `isOnAdventure` and `counters` for every zone. It sends **no** `exiledWith`, `untilLeavesBattlefield`, `exiledCards`, `foretold`, `mayPlay` or playable-zone list. Playability arrives only as `cast`/`activateAbility` actions inside a `chooseAction` prompt, which exists only while you hold priority. The vendored copy in this project is **older than upstream** and lacks `isSuspended` and `isOnAdventure`. The project's CardDto reference does list `controllerId`, `ownerId`, `isCopy`, `isTransformed`, `isBestowed`, `phasedOut`, `attachedTo`/`attachmentIds`, `isPlotted`, `isWarpExiled` and `isMadnessExiled` ([engine-interface-reference.md](file:///docs/engine-interface-reference.md)). Two engine questions are unverified. It is not confirmed that Forge's `Dig` effect, used by Gonti, Light Up the Stage and hideaway, calls `handleExiledWith`, so `getExiledWith()` may be empty for those cards. Nobody has checked how Forge prompts the CR 903.9b command-zone replacement.

## Conclusion

The rules split exile into three independent facts that paper players carry in their heads and that the bridge currently drops. The first is a **link** to an object instance, which ends on a zone change. The second is a **permission** held by a player, which ends on a clock or when the card leaves exile. The third is a **marker** on the card itself, which ends on a zone change of the card. These facts vary independently: a Gonti card has a permission and no live link, an Arcane Bombardment card has a link and no permission once orphaned, and a Dauthi card has only a marker. A single "exiled-with" arrow therefore can't stand in for all three. The engine already distinguishes them, so the table should mirror that three-way model rather than invent its own.

Most of the hard rules logic, such as when a return happens, whether it uses the stack, owner routing and the commander choice, already runs inside Forge and needs no Lua. The real risk is information that never leaves the Java harness. Faithful exposure therefore matters more than clever rendering. A handful of `CardDto` fields and an every-snapshot playable list would turn the trickiest Commander interactions, from Valki and Tibalt to Hostage Taker, Banishing Light and Gonti, from invisible engine state into things players can see on the table. Two unverified engine paths, `Dig` linking and the 903.9b prompt, should be checked before that work is built on them.
