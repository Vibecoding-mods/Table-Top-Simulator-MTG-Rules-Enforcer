## How the new effects are meant to be played

**Wipe wave** (`wipe_wave`, `death_wipe_W/U/B/R/G/C/M`): one board-wide element, then the small per-card `death` (or `death_burn`) per card, staggered 0.1-0.2 s, from the table's Lua.
- At rig scale 1 the band (2.6 deep) is 24 units long along the rig's local x (one seat's battlefield, 10 columns of 2.4), centred on the rig, and sweeps the rig's local **+z** about 17 units in 0.35 s, with a thin bright leading edge 1.1 ahead and a mist wake that trails behind it (gone by 0.6 s).
- Put the rig at the edge of the board on the spell's side, turned so +z points across the board. Hierarchy scaling: `setScale(2)` doubles the band, the travel and the thickness (a whole-table sweep). Reset the scale when the rig is parked or reused, since the other effects scale with it too.
- `death_wipe` is still the shipped seat-wide cloud (purple), for the Lua that plays it today.

**Tint path**: `wipe_wave` and `exile_neutral` are white and grey, so `setColorTint(c)` on the rig gives colour `c` if TTS's tint reaches the particle materials' `_Color` (unverified; the spec's probe 1). A tint would colour every effect on that rig, so the Lua would set it before each play and back to white after. If tint doesn't reach them, use the baked `death_wipe_<key>` and `exile`.

**Death flavours**: `death` (destroyed: shatter) and `death_burn` (died from damage: burn-away).

**Bolt** (`mtg-bolt.unity3d`, its own small rig type, GM notes to be decided by the Lua):
1. Put the rig at the source (the spell's stack spot, or the ability's card), turn it so its local **+z** points at the target (yaw and pitch), and `setScale(d)` uniformly, with d = the distance to the target.
2. `playTriggerEffect(<bolt>)` at least `RIG_LEAD` later. The head flies local z 0 to 1 (the target) in 0.25 s, speeding up, on a slight arc (peak 0.1 x d above the line, at mid-flight), with a world-space ribbon trail and embers; the impact (white-hot flash, a coloured ring opening to about 2.75 units, sparks) starts at 0.25 s at the target; the effect is 0.6 s long.
3. Schedule the target's damage, hit or death effect at play time + 0.25 s. Reset the rig's scale when it is parked (its 0.2 box collider scales too; the rig is not interactable).
- The head's move is a **legacy `Animation`** clip `bolt_head` on the child `head`, named in the trigger effect's `Animation` field (`AnimationComponent` + `AnimationName`), as `TTSAssetBundleEffects` supports. The component also has `playAutomatically` on, so it plays when TTS turns the effect's object on, even if TTS ignores the Animation field. Whether either happens in real TTS is the spec's probe 2. If the head stays at the source (only an impact at the target and a short smudge at the source), the Animation doesn't play: use the fallback.
- **Fallback** `bolt_trail`: the head, trail and embers at the rig itself, no animation, 0.6 s of trail. Lua moves the rig from the source to the target (`setPositionSmooth`, or per-frame `setPosition`), then plays `bolt_impact` (the impact alone at the rig, 0.35 s) on arrival, or `damage` from `mtg-fx`.
- Rings (`counter`, `counter_minus`, `exile_neutral`, the bolt's impact ring) lie flat in the rig's plane: as billboards the table cut them in half.
- Every bolt system uses **Local scaling mode**: the rig's scale stretches the path but never a particle's size or speed (the check below).
