# Readability audit A: states on a permanent

Scope: per-permanent statuses a player needs to see on the battlefield. Read-only audit, 2026-11-12. Prepared is excluded (another agent).

**Status 2026-11-27:** gaps 1 ("Doesn't untap next untap step", `skipsNextUntap`), 2 ("Exerted (won't untap)"), 4 (Monstrous), 5 (Saddled, Harnessed), 7 (Detained until ...) and 8 ("Regeneration shield") are built (`statusLists` in `bridge/bridge.js`; search the strings). The line numbers below are from 2026-11-12 and have moved. Gaps 3 and 6 were not re-checked.

Pipeline checked: Forge `Card.java` -> harness `InteractiveSnapshotExtractor.toCard` (manabrew/forge-harness/src/main/java/forge/harness/host/InteractiveSnapshotExtractor.java:755-898, the only fields the bridge can ever see) -> `tableCard` (bridge/bridge.js:1809-1896) -> `badgeParts` (tools/engine-renderer.lua:303-385: COPY chip, "tb" text-changed box, one green "kw" status box, counters box, P/T badge) and `description` (engine-renderer.lua:73-79, hover text).

The only per-permanent status channel is the green "kw" status box, fed by `statusLists` (bridge.js:1881-1892) plus granted keywords from `cardKeywords` (bridge.js:2031-2050). Anything that is not a Forge keyword string, a DTO flag, or a bridge text-parsed set (`game.goaded/renowned/suspected/solved`) is invisible on the card.

## Already fine (code-verified)

- Phased out: `po` flag (bridge.js:1878), dimmed card plus "PHASED OUT" status box (engine-renderer.lua:354-355, tint at 289, 498).
- Goaded by whom: "Goaded by <player>" (bridge.js:1881), set from resolved goad text, cleared on the goader's next turn (3306-3308).
- Suspected: "Suspected: can't block" (bridge.js:1883), tracked from resolved text (3322-3340). Menace appears through granted keywords.
- Renowned: "Renowned" (bridge.js:1882, set at 3379-3385).
- Exerted during the turn it is exerted (bridge.js:1882); see gap 2 for the later turns.
- Crewed this turn (bridge.js:1886); becomes-a-creature logs (bridge.js:2968). Vehicle types switch to Creature and P/T appears.
- Bestowed, Ring-bearer (bridge.js:1886), haunted (1884), summoning sick on active player's creatures (1885).
- Saga chapter and next chapter text (bridge.js:1887, 1967-1972); Class level and next level cost (1888, 1975-1978).
- Attached Aura/Equipment: `att` and attachment placing beside the host (engine-renderer.lua:2652-2680, 6191 for linked exile).
- Copy: "COPY" chip (engine-renderer.lua:319, bridge.js:1877) and "<copy name>, as <real name>" in hover (engine-renderer.lua:74, bridge.js:1875).
- Text changes (Mind Bend, Exchange of Words): "tb" box (engine-renderer.lua:323-336).
- Controller changed: "Owned by <player>" (bridge.js:1891).
- Counters: +1/+1 and -1/-1 netted, others by name, loyalty and defense as the P/T badge (engine-renderer.lua:359-383); stun, lore, oil, shield named.
- Pumped/damaged power and toughness vs printed base, with a lowered/raised color (engine-renderer.lua:371-382); face-down base is 2/2 (bridge.js:1825).
- Tapped and attacking: `atk`, `atkT`, `blk`, `blkOf` fields (bridge.js:1863-1864), "Dies in this combat as it stands" (1890).
- Granted keywords, hexproof/protection from X, landwalk (cardKeywords, bridge.js:2031-2050) in the status box; own always-on keywords too (ALWAYS_ON, 2015).
- Choices on entry (color, type, named card, player, mode, number): `choiceWords` (bridge.js:1892, 1993-2004).
- Foretold (`fo`), plotted/madness/warp notes in exile (cardNotes, bridge.js:1909-1922), linked exile (`ew`, "comes back when ... leaves", engine-renderer.lua:76).
- Commander tax: cost badge in the command zone (bridge.js:1947, docs/cost-badge-spec.md).
- Dungeon room and progress: `dg` badge (engine-renderer.lua:316).
- Day/night, monarch, initiative, poison, ring level: player-level status pieces (docs/status-objects-spec.md), out of scope here.

## Top 8 (by harm)

1. Face-down permanents unreadable to their own controller (gap 3), M.
2. "Doesn't untap during next untap step" keyword dropped by `cardKeywords` (gap 1), S, bridge only.
3. Exert badge gone from cleanup on (gap 2), S, one engine line.
4. Mutate stack contents never shown (gap 6), M, bridge + Lua.
5. Dash/Blitz/Unearth/Encore end-of-turn riders not on the permanent (gap 10), M.
6. Temporary can't block/attack/must attack not on the creature (gap 9), M.
7. Monstrous and Saddled (gaps 4, 5), S each, engine flags.
8. Detained and regeneration shield exist in bridge state but never reach the card (gaps 7, 8), S, bridge only.
