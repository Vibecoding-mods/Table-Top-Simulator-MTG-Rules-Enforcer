New since November 9, 2026

What's on the Table

Everything players can see or use that was added to our rules-enforced Commander table in Tabletop Simulator from November 9 to November 13. The Forge rules engine runs the game: you play with the table as usual, and it knows the rules, asks the questions and moves the cards.

New ways to play

  • Planechase: planes sit in the middle of the table, with the planar die (first roll free, then {1}, {2}…).
  • Dungeons: venture into a dungeon and the current room shows in its own box.
  • AI seats by colour: pick which seats the Forge AI takes and which deck each one plays.
  • Free mulligans button on the table's rim: one shared setting (None, 1 or 2) anyone can change. Anyone seated can also turn the engine on and start the game.
  • Your own card art: put a picture link after a card in your deck list (1 Sol Ring #https://…png) and every copy wears it for everyone. Steam Cloud links work too. The host can turn it off with mtg art off.
  • Foreign-language printings in your deck list now resolve to the English card.

Playing your cards

  • Double-faced cards (Valki // Tibalt and friends): see the other side and cast from the side shown.
  • Token stacks the Arena way: five or more identical tokens become one card with an ×N label; Split lays them out when you need just some.
  • Cost badge: when a spell costs more or less than printed (tax, reducers), a counter over its mana cost shows the real price.
  • Play-from row: cards you may play from exile or your graveyard (flashback, foretell, adventure…) wait in a glowing row beside your hand.
  • Lands snap into your mat's mana box; any other card stays exactly where you drop it.
  • Planeswalkers by click: activate loyalty abilities from the card, with the [+2] / [−3] costs read right.
  • Green glow on your permanents that have an ability you can use right now (only you see it).
  • Big stacks: "Resolve all" through your own triggers, or "Resolve N×" for a pile of the same trigger.
  • A refused play says why: the rule, the zone, a missing target or the mana you're short.

Combat

  • Clearer combat: attackers and blockers in each player's colour, with tabs and block confirmations.
  • Attack reminders: attack taxes per defender (Propaganda, Ghostly Prison), who can't attack and why, and "attack whom?" when there's a choice.
  • A refused attack names the limit it broke (for example "no more than 2 creatures can attack AI 1").
  • The attacker gets priority after blockers when there's something to do.

Reading the game

  • Status pieces: the Monarch's crown, Initiative, City's Blessing, the Ring, and Day // Night.
  • Player marks as small boxes under your life heart (poison, energy, experience…).
  • EXTRA TURN badge beside your life (2×, 3× when they stack).
  • A clearer game log: what caused each death or life change, a short version of each ability, and names only when the engine is sure.
  • More on the board: your own face-down cards, what a card exiled, foretell and suspend counts, partner commander damage, extra combat phases, skipped turns, opponents' floating mana.
  • New engine panel: one dock of tabs in the table's colours, and an ability panel that matches the engine.
  • Game over: a VICTORY or DEFEAT banner, then who won.
  • Scoop now shows up in games against the AI too.

Look and sound

  • Particle effects: deaths break apart in place, a board wipe sends a wave in the spell's colour, exiled cards collapse into light, and damage spells fly a bolt to their target. mtg fx on / low / off (host).
  • Table sounds for deaths, board wipes, exile, damage, big hits, life gain, instants and sorceries (when they resolve), counterspells, and Treasure made and spent. Several at once stack into one bigger sound.
  • A COUNTERED pop-up when your spell is countered.
  • A new background with the floor pulled closer.

For the host

  • MTG Companion: one double-click starts Docker, the engine, the bridge and TTS, with a status page in your browser. Every button explains itself on hover.
  • It heals itself after Docker or WSL restarts and trims old logs every two weeks. Self-updates from GitHub are built and switch on once the project is shared.
  • A Linux / macOS version of the companion (waiting on its first Steam Deck test).
  • Illustrated guides for the companion and the table.

Being built now

Not on the table yet: built or in progress, still to be tested.

  • Planechase polish: chaos triggers labelled, "leaves the game" lines, clearer planeswalk questions.
  • GIF frames: a small frame on your mat that plays a GIF you search for.
  • Every kind of two-sided card: transform, meld, flip, split, adventure and more.
  • Standard mode: 60-card decks at 20 life.
  • Two-Headed Giant: 2v2 with shared life and a shared turn.
  • Pick your starting hand when testing against the AI.

Some of these are brand new and haven't been through many real games yet, so tell the host if something looks off. Only the host runs the engine; everyone else just needs Tabletop Simulator.
